Vocabulary List 51: PR-QU Words

1. PromulgateSSAT vocabularyTo make widely known or officially announce.
2. PropensitySSAT vocabularyA natural tendency.

4. ProponentSSAT vocabularyA supporter or advocate.

6. ProprietySSAT vocabularyProper behavior or social correctness.
7. ProsaicSSAT vocabularyDull, ordinary, or unimaginative.
8. ProscribeSSAT vocabularyTo forbid or ban.

10. ProtagonistSSAT vocabularyThe main character or leading supporter.
11. ProtractSSAT vocabularyTo prolong or draw out.
12. ProvidentSSAT vocabularyCareful in planning for the future.
13. ProvincialSSAT vocabularyNarrow-minded or limited in outlook.
14. ProvisionalSSAT vocabularyTemporary and subject to change.
15. ProximitySSAT vocabularyNearness.
16. PrudentSSAT vocabularyWise and careful.
17. PurportedSSAT vocabularyClaimed or alleged.
18. PutativeSSAT vocabularyGenerally supposed or assumed.
19. QualifySSAT vocabularyTo meet requirements; or limit a statement.
20. QualitativeSSAT vocabularyRelated to qualities rather than numbers.
