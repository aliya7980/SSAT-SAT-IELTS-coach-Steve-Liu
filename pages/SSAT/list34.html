Vocabulary List 34: EX-FL Words

1. ExtrovertSSAT vocabularyAn outgoing, socially expressive person.

5. FacetiousSSAT vocabularyTreating serious matters with joking or inappropriate humor.
6. FacileSSAT vocabularyAppearing easy or smooth but lacking depth.
7. FactionSSAT vocabularyA small dissenting group within a larger one.
8. FarcicalSSAT vocabularyAbsurd, ridiculous, or like a farce.

20. FlagrantSSAT vocabularyObviously and shockingly wrong.
