Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In one busy school week, every conversation seemed to introduce a new vocabulary word. The library was built through the donor's Largess. Once the ballot was submitted, the vote was Irrevocable. His Laconic reply was simply, "I agree." The principal paused to Laud the students for helping younger readers. The Judicious choice was to save money before buying new equipment.
