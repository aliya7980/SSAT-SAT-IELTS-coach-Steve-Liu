Vocabulary List 30: DI-DI Words

1. DictumSSAT vocabularyA formal statement, rule, or authoritative saying.
2. DidacticSSAT vocabularyIntended to teach, often in a moralizing way.
3. PedanticSSAT vocabularyOverly concerned with small rules or bookish details.
4. DiffidentSSAT vocabularyShy and lacking self-confidence.
5. TimidSSAT vocabularyShy, fearful, or easily frightened.
6. DigressSSAT vocabularyTo wander away from the main topic.
7. DilettanteSSAT vocabularyA person with a shallow amateur interest in an art or field.
8. DiscernSSAT vocabularyTo perceive, recognize, or distinguish clearly.
9. DiscreetSSAT vocabularyCareful, tactful, and avoiding embarrassment.
10. DiscreteSSAT vocabularySeparate and distinct.
11. DiscriminateSSAT vocabularyTo recognize differences; or to treat unfairly based on category.
12. DisdainSSAT vocabularyA feeling of scorn or strong dislike.
13. DisinterestedSSAT vocabularyImpartial and not influenced by personal gain.
14. DisparageSSAT vocabularyTo belittle or speak of as unimportant.
15. DisparateSSAT vocabularyEssentially different or not comparable.
16. DisseminateSSAT vocabularyTo spread information widely.
17. DissipateSSAT vocabularyTo scatter, waste, or gradually disappear.
18. DissolutionSSAT vocabularyThe breaking up, ending, or decay of something.
19. DistendSSAT vocabularyTo swell or expand from pressure inside.
20. DistinguishSSAT vocabularyTo tell apart or recognize as different.
