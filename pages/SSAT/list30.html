Vocabulary List 30: DI-DI Words

5. TimidSSAT vocabularyShy, fearful, or easily frightened.
6. DigressSSAT vocabularyTo wander away from the main topic.
7. DilettanteSSAT vocabularyA person with a shallow amateur interest in an art or field.

14. DisparageSSAT vocabularyTo belittle or speak of as unimportant.

16. DisseminateSSAT vocabularyTo spread information widely.

19. DistendSSAT vocabularyTo swell or expand from pressure inside.
20. DistinguishSSAT vocabularyTo tell apart or recognize as different.
