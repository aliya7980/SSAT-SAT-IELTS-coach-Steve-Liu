Vocabulary List 56: SP-ST Words

1. SpawnSSAT vocabularyTo produce or give rise to.
2. SpeciousSSAT vocabularySeeming true but actually false.
3. PlausibleSSAT vocabularySeeming reasonable or believable.
4. CharlatanSSAT vocabularyA fraud pretending to have special knowledge.
5. SpuriousSSAT vocabularyFalse or not genuine.
6. ApocryphalSSAT vocabularyOf doubtful authenticity; probably false.
7. SqualorSSAT vocabularyFilthy and miserable conditions.
8. RepulsiveSSAT vocabularyDisgusting or causing strong dislike.
9. InsurgencySSAT vocabularyAn organized rebellion.
10. SquanderSSAT vocabularyTo waste carelessly.
11. StagnationSSAT vocabularyA lack of movement, growth, or progress.
12. StaticSSAT vocabularyNot changing or moving.
13. SoarSSAT vocabularyTo rise high or increase rapidly.
14. StaunchSSAT vocabularyFirmly loyal or dependable.
15. SteadfastSSAT vocabularyFirm, loyal, and unwavering.
16. StigmatizeSSAT vocabularyTo mark with disgrace or shame.
17. StipulateSSAT vocabularyTo specify as a condition.
18. StoicSSAT vocabularyCalm and enduring pain without complaint.
19. StatumSSAT vocabularyA level or layer; likely intended as stratum.
20. StrictureSSAT vocabularyA restriction or severe criticism.
