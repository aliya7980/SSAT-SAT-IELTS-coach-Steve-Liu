Vocabulary List 57: ST-SY Words

1. StrifeSSAT vocabularyConflict or bitter disagreement.
2. StringentSSAT vocabularyStrict and demanding.
3. StymieSSAT vocabularyTo block or hinder.
4. ThwartSSAT vocabularyTo prevent from succeeding.
5. SubjugateSSAT vocabularyTo bring under control or domination.
6. SublimeSSAT vocabularyExtremely beautiful, noble, or awe-inspiring.
7. ExaltedSSAT vocabularyRaised in rank, power, or dignity.
8. SubordinateSSAT vocabularyLower in rank or importance.
9. SubstantiveSSAT vocabularyImportant, real, and meaningful.
10. SubtleSSAT vocabularyDelicate, slight, or not obvious.
11. SubversiveSSAT vocabularySeeking to undermine an established system.
12. SuccinctSSAT vocabularyBrief and clearly expressed.
13. SuccumbSSAT vocabularyTo give in or yield.
14. Supercilious/ haughty/ patronizingSSAT vocabularyActing arrogantly superior toward others.
15. SuperficialSSAT vocabularyShallow or concerned only with the surface.
16. SuperfluousSSAT vocabularyMore than needed; unnecessary.
17. SurfeitSSAT vocabularyAn excessive amount.
18. SurreptitiousSSAT vocabularySecretive, especially because improper.
19. SurrogateSSAT vocabularyA substitute.
20. SycophantSSAT vocabularyA flatterer who seeks advantage.
