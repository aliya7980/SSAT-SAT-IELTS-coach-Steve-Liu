One complete module for BA, BE, and BL words.

Vocabulary List 14–16

1. BanaladjectiveCommonplace, predictable, or lacking originality.
2. BanenounA cause of great trouble, harm, or ruin.
3. BastionnounA stronghold or place that protects a principle or group.
4. BegetverbTo cause, produce, or give rise to.
5. BelaborverbTo explain or discuss repeatedly and excessively.
6. BeleaguerverbTo trouble persistently or surround with difficulties.
7. BelieverbTo contradict, disguise, or give a false impression of.
8. BelittleverbTo make someone or something seem unimportant.
9. BelligerentadjectiveHostile, aggressive, and eager to fight.
10. BemusedadjectiveConfused or puzzled.
11. BenefactornounA person who gives help, especially money.
12. BenevolentadjectiveKind, generous, and wishing to do good.
13. BenignadjectiveHarmless, gentle, or not dangerous.
14. BequestnounMoney or property left to someone in a will.
15. BereavedadjectiveGrieving after the death of a loved one.
16. BesetverbTo trouble or attack from all sides.
17. BlasphemynounSpeech or action showing disrespect for what is sacred.
18. BlatantadjectiveCompletely obvious, often in an offensive way.
19. Blightnoun/verbA disease or destructive influence; to damage severely.
20. BlitheadjectiveCheerful and carefree, sometimes without proper concern.

Exercise 3: Paragraph Using All Words

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

A benevolent benefactor left a generous bequest to restore an old library, long considered a bastion of learning. The donor's bereaved niece looked bemused when a belligerent critic began to belittle the plan. His blatant accusations seemed to belie the project's careful research. He continued to belabor a banal complaint and even called the restoration an act of blasphemy. The committee, already beset by rising costs and delays that continued to beleaguer it, refused to let his hostility become the bane of the project. A plant blight had damaged the courtyard, but experts confirmed that a stain inside the building was benign. Meanwhile, several blithe volunteers worked with cheerful confidence. Their effort would beget renewed hope for the entire neighborhood.
