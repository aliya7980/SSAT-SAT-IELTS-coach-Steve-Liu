SSAT Vocabulary Practice: List 59

Twenty-word practice set from the Ubiquitous Essentials SSAT/SAT academic glossary.

Vocabulary List 59: TO-VE Words

1. ToutSSAT vocabularyTo praise or promote strongly.
2. TranscendSSAT vocabularyTo rise above or go beyond limits.
3. TransgressSSAT vocabularyTo violate a law or boundary.
4. TransientSSAT vocabularyTemporary or short-lived.
5. TrepidationSSAT vocabularyFearful uncertainty or anxiety.
6. TurpitudeSSAT vocabularyMoral corruption or wickedness.
7. UbiquitousSSAT vocabularyPresent or found everywhere.
8. UnconscionableSSAT vocabularyShockingly unfair or unreasonable.
9. UnctuousSSAT vocabularyExcessively flattering or oily in manner.
10. UniformSSAT vocabularyConsistent; the same throughout.
11. UnremittingSSAT vocabularyNever stopping or slackening.
12. UnwittingSSAT vocabularyNot aware; unintentional.
13. UrbaneSSAT vocabularyPolished, refined, and courteous.
14. UsurpSSAT vocabularyTo seize power or position unlawfully.
15. UtilitarianSSAT vocabularyPractical and useful rather than decorative.
16. UtopiaSSAT vocabularyAn imagined perfect society.
17. VacillateSSAT vocabularyTo waver between choices.
18. VapidSSAT vocabularyDull and lacking liveliness.
19. VehementSSAT vocabularyIntense, forceful, or passionate.
20. VenalSSAT vocabularyCorruptible, especially by bribery.
