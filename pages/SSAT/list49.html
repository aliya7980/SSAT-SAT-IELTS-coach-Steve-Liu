Vocabulary List 49: PL-PR Words

3. PlatitudeSSAT vocabularyA dull, overused remark.
4. PlebeianSSAT vocabularyCommon or ordinary; of the lower class.
5. DiscriminatingSSAT vocabularyShowing good taste or careful judgment.
6. PoignantSSAT vocabularyDeeply moving or touching.
7. PolarizeSSAT vocabularyTo divide into opposing groups.
8. PolemicSSAT vocabularyA strong written or spoken attack.
9. PonderousSSAT vocabularyHeavy, slow, or dull.

12. PostulateSSAT vocabularyTo assume as true for argument.
13. AxiomSSAT vocabularyA self-evident truth or accepted principle.
14. PragmaticSSAT vocabularyPractical rather than idealistic.
15. PrecedentSSAT vocabularyAn earlier example used as a guide.
16. PreceptSSAT vocabularyA rule or principle for behavior.

19. PrecludeSSAT vocabularyTo prevent or make impossible.
20. PrecursorSSAT vocabularySomething that comes before and signals another.
