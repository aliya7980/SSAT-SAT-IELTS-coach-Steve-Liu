Twenty-word practice set from the Ubiquitous Essentials SSAT/SAT academic glossary.

Vocabulary List 29: DE-DI Words

1. DeferenceSSAT vocabularyRespectful submission to another person's judgment or wishes.
2. DefinitiveSSAT vocabularyFinal, authoritative, and not needing further change.
3. DegenerateSSAT vocabularyTo decline in quality, morals, or condition.
4. DeleteriousSSAT vocabularyHarmful or damaging.
5. DelineateSSAT vocabularyTo describe, outline, or portray clearly.
6. DeludeSSAT vocabularyTo mislead or deceive someone.
7. DelugeSSAT vocabularyA flood or overwhelming amount.
8. DemagogueSSAT vocabularyA leader who appeals to fear or prejudice rather than reason.
9. DenizenSSAT vocabularyAn inhabitant or regular resident of a place.
10. DepravitySSAT vocabularyMoral corruption or wickedness.
11. DeprecateSSAT vocabularyTo express disapproval of or criticize mildly.
12. DerideSSAT vocabularyTo mock or ridicule.
13. DerogatorySSAT vocabularyInsulting or showing disrespect.
14. DesiccateSSAT vocabularyTo dry out completely.
15. DespondentSSAT vocabularyDeeply discouraged or hopeless.
16. DespotSSAT vocabularyA ruler with absolute, often cruel, power.
17. DestituteSSAT vocabularyExtremely poor or lacking necessities.
18. DesultorySSAT vocabularyLacking a plan, purpose, or steady connection.
19. DextrousSSAT vocabularySkillful and quick with the hands or mind.
20. DialecticalSSAT vocabularyRelated to logical discussion of opposing ideas.
