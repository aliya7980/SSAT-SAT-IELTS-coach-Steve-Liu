Vocabulary List 31: DO-EM Words

1. DocileSSAT vocabularyEasily taught, led, or managed.
2. DoctrinaireSSAT vocabularyStubbornly devoted to a theory regardless of practicality.
3. DogmaticSSAT vocabularyAssertive of opinions as if they were unquestionable facts.
4. DomesticSSAT vocabularyRelated to home, family, or one's own country.
5. DormantSSAT vocabularyInactive but capable of becoming active.
6. DubiousSSAT vocabularyDoubtful or uncertain.
7. DuplicitySSAT vocabularyDeceitfulness or double-dealing.
8. EbullientSSAT vocabularyCheerful, enthusiastic, and full of energy.
9. EccentricSSAT vocabularyUnconventional or oddly different.
10. EclecticSSAT vocabularyDrawn from a variety of sources or styles.
11. EdifySSAT vocabularyTo instruct or improve morally or intellectually.
12. EffaceSSAT vocabularyTo erase, wipe out, or make less noticeable.
13. EffusionSSAT vocabularyAn unrestrained expression of feeling.
14. EgalitarianSSAT vocabularyBelieving in equal rights and opportunities.
15. EgocentricSSAT vocabularySelf-centered; seeing everything from one's own view.
16. EgregiousSSAT vocabularyOutstandingly bad or shocking.
17. ElicitSSAT vocabularyTo draw out a response or information.
18. EllipticalSSAT vocabularyIndirect, obscure, or leaving words out.
19. ElusiveSSAT vocabularyHard to catch, find, or understand.
20. EmigrateSSAT vocabularyTo leave one's country to live elsewhere.
