Vocabulary List 58: SY-TO Words

1. SynthesisSSAT vocabularyA combination of parts into a whole.
2. TacitSSAT vocabularyUnderstood without being directly stated.
3. TaciturnSSAT vocabularyQuiet and not talkative.
4. TangentialSSAT vocabularyOnly slightly related; going off topic.
5. Tangible/ palpableSSAT vocabularyClear enough to touch, feel, or notice.
6. TantamountSSAT vocabularyEquivalent in effect or value.
7. TautologicalSSAT vocabularyNeedlessly repetitive in meaning.
8. TemeritySSAT vocabularyReckless boldness.
9. AudacitySSAT vocabularyBold courage or rude boldness.
10. TemperateSSAT vocabularyModerate and self-controlled.
11. TenableSSAT vocabularyAble to be defended or maintained.
12. TenaciousSSAT vocabularyPersistent and not giving up.
13. TenetSSAT vocabularyA principle or belief.
14. TentativeSSAT vocabularyUncertain or not final.
15. TenuousSSAT vocabularyWeak, thin, or poorly supported.
16. TerseSSAT vocabularyBrief and direct.
17. TheologySSAT vocabularyThe study of religion and God.
18. TiradeSSAT vocabularyA long angry speech.
19. TorporSSAT vocabularyA state of sluggishness or inactivity.
20. TouchstoneSSAT vocabularyA standard used to judge quality.

Exercise 3: Paragraph Using All Words

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In one busy school week, every conversation seemed to introduce a new vocabulary word. Ignoring the warning was Tantamount to accepting the risk. There was a Tacit agreement that nobody would mention the mistake. His comment was Tangential and pulled the discussion away from the topic. The phrase "free gift" is Tautological. Kindness became the club's central Tenet. A Temperate response helped cool the argument. The argument was Tenuous because it depended on one rumor. The Tenacious reporter kept asking questions until the truth emerged. His Terse email said only, "Meeting moved to noon." The coach's Tirade after the loss lasted ten minutes. The claim was Tenable because the evidence supported it. The Taciturn student listened carefully but rarely spoke. Her Audacity impressed some people and shocked others. Honesty became the Touchstone for choosing team leaders. The college offered a course in Theology. The excitement in the gym was almost Tangible/ palpable. The schedule was Tentative until the gym was reserved. The final essay was a Synthesis of research, interviews, and personal insight. It took Temerity to interrupt the principal during assembly. After lunch, the room fell into Torpor.
