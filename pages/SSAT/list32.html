SSAT Vocabulary Practice: List 32

Twenty-word practice set from the Ubiquitous Essentials SSAT/SAT academic glossary.

Vocabulary List 32: EM-ET Words

1. EminentSSAT vocabularyFamous, respected, or outstanding.
2. EmpiricalSSAT vocabularyBased on observation or experience rather than theory alone.
3. EmulateSSAT vocabularyTo imitate with the goal of equaling or surpassing.
4. EncroachSSAT vocabularyTo intrude gradually on another's rights or space.
5. EndemicSSAT vocabularyNative to or regularly found in a particular place.
6. EnervateSSAT vocabularyTo weaken or drain energy from.
7. EnfranchiseSSAT vocabularyTo give voting rights or full membership rights.
8. EngenderSSAT vocabularyTo cause or give rise to.
9. EnigmaSSAT vocabularyA mystery or puzzling person or thing.
10. EnormitySSAT vocabularyGreat wickedness; or something shocking in scale.
11. EphemeralSSAT vocabularyLasting only a short time.
12. EpigramSSAT vocabularyA brief, clever, memorable saying.
13. EpitomeSSAT vocabularyA perfect example of a quality or type.
14. EquanimitySSAT vocabularyCalmness and composure, especially under stress.
15. EquitableSSAT vocabularyFair and just.
16. EquivocalSSAT vocabularyAmbiguous or open to more than one interpretation.
17. EruditeSSAT vocabularyVery learned or scholarly.
18. EsotericSSAT vocabularyUnderstood by only a small, specialized group.
19. EspouseSSAT vocabularyTo support, adopt, or advocate.
20. EtherealSSAT vocabularyDelicate, light, and seemingly not of this world.

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In one busy school week, every conversation seemed to introduce a new vocabulary word. The missing key became an Enigma that the whole class wanted to solve. The award went to an Eminent scientist whose research changed modern medicine. The science fair judges wanted Empirical evidence from experiments, not guesses. The rainbow was Ephemeral, fading before anyone could take a photo. The Erudite professor could explain ancient history without notes. That rare flower is Endemic to the island and grows nowhere else. The new parking lot began to Encroach on the small public garden. A week of poor sleep can Enervate even the most energetic student.
