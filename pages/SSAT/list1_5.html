Exercise 3: Paragraph Using All Words

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

After an abortive attempt to hold the debate outdoors, the club faced abysmal attendance and an abject mood. The chair refused to abdicate her position or use absolute authority to silence criticism. With acute observation and impressive acumen, she recognized that the failure was an aberration, not a permanent pattern. She did not want to abash the volunteers, although she did abhor careless planning. Instead, she offered to abridge the most abstract and abstruse section of the lecture. One member chose to abnegate social media and remain abstinent from online arguments, but an acerbic critic still tried to accost him. An acrid smell from a nearby kitchen made their acrimonious exchange worse. The adviser asked both students to acquiesce and wait for their anger to abate. After the successful second event, the school agreed to absolve the team of blame and present the chair with an accolade.

Exercise 3: Paragraph Using All Words

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In an agrarian town, an affluent donor proposed a new community arts center. The mayor placed the proposal at the top of the meeting's agenda and was adamant that the council should address the town's needs before voting. An adherent of the project responded with such alacrity that several residents volunteered immediately. The adroit architect presented an aesthetic design featuring a sculpture made from a durable alloy. Her affable manner reflected a genuine affinity for local history rather than an artificial affectation. Although the crowd offered enthusiastic adulation, one resident began to allege that the builders planned to adulterate the materials with cheaper substances. The mayor had to admonish him for making an unsupported accusation. Even an agnostic council member, who was uncertain about the donor's motives, agreed that evidence was necessary. To alleviate public concern, the council chose to allocate funds for independent testing. The aggregate cost remained manageable, and despite adverse weather, construction began on time.
