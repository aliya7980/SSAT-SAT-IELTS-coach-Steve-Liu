Vocabulary List 35: FL-GE Words

1. FlauntSSAT vocabularyTo show off boldly.

3. FoibleSSAT vocabularyA minor weakness or eccentric habit.
4. FomentSSAT vocabularyTo stir up or encourage trouble.
5. ForbearSSAT vocabularyTo refrain from doing something; to be patient.
6. ForegoSSAT vocabularyTo go before; often used to mean give up.
7. ForsakeSSAT vocabularyTo abandon or give up.
8. FortuitousSSAT vocabularyHappening by chance, often luckily.
9. FounderSSAT vocabularyTo fail, collapse, or sink.
10. FraternalSSAT vocabularyBrotherly or related to a brotherhood.
11. FreneticSSAT vocabularyWildly energetic or frantic.
12. FrugalSSAT vocabularyCareful with money or resources.
13. FurtiveSSAT vocabularySecretive or stealthy.
14. FutileSSAT vocabularyUseless or unlikely to succeed.
15. GarrulousSSAT vocabularyExcessively talkative.
16. GaucheSSAT vocabularyAwkward, tactless, or socially clumsy.
17. MaladroitSSAT vocabularyClumsy or lacking skill.
18. DextrousSSAT vocabularySkillful and quick with the hands or mind.
19. GenreSSAT vocabularyA category or type of art, music, or literature.
20. GenteelSSAT vocabularyPolite, refined, or trying to appear upper-class.

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In one busy school week, every conversation seemed to introduce a new vocabulary word. Our meeting at the airport was Fortuitous, but it changed everything. The classroom became Frenetic five minutes before winter break. Forgetting names was his harmless Foible. A Frugal shopper compares prices before buying shoes. To save money, we had to Forego dessert for a week. The student gave a Furtive glance at the hidden note. Please Forbear from talking while others are presenting. One careless rumor can Foment an argument that lasts for days. Mystery is her favorite literary Genre. He liked to Flaunt his new phone even during class. The teammates developed a Fraternal bond after months of practice. The plan began to Founder when no one remembered the supplies. A loyal friend would not Forsake you during a hard time. The host spoke in a Genteel manner to every guest. His Gauche joke made the dinner table go silent. The driver chose to Flout the parking rule. His first attempt at juggling was Maladroit, but he laughed with everyone else. The Garrulous tour guide described every brick in the building. It was Futile to argue after the decision had already been made. The pianist was so Dextrous that her fingers seemed to fly over the keys.
