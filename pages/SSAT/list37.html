Twenty-word practice set from the Ubiquitous Essentials SSAT/SAT academic glossary.

Vocabulary List 37: HE-IM Words

1. HermeticSSAT vocabularyAirtight; or isolated from outside influence.
2. ImperviousSSAT vocabularyNot allowing passage; unaffected by something.
3. HeydaySSAT vocabularyThe period of greatest success or power.
4. HiatusSSAT vocabularyA pause, gap, or break.
5. HierarchySSAT vocabularyA ranked system of authority or importance.
6. HistrionicSSAT vocabularyOverly dramatic or theatrical.
7. Homily= sermonSSAT vocabularyA moral or religious lecture; a sermon.
8. HomogenousSSAT vocabularyOf the same kind throughout.
9. HeterogeneousSSAT vocabularyMade up of different kinds.
10. HusbandrySSAT vocabularyCareful management of resources, especially farming.
11. HyperboleSSAT vocabularyDeliberate exaggeration for effect.
12. HypotheticalSSAT vocabularyBased on a possible idea or assumption.
13. IconoclastSSAT vocabularyOne who attacks cherished beliefs or institutions.
14. IdeologySSAT vocabularyA system of ideas and beliefs.
15. IdiosyncrasySSAT vocabularyA distinctive personal habit or trait.
16. Idyllic= rusticSSAT vocabularyPeaceful, simple, and charming like country life.
17. IgnominySSAT vocabularyPublic shame or disgrace.
18. IllicitSSAT vocabularyIllegal or forbidden.
19. ImmigrateSSAT vocabularyTo come into a country to live there.
20. ImminentSSAT vocabularyAbout to happen soon.
