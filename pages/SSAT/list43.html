Vocabulary List 43: MA-MA Words

7. MaudlinSSAT vocabularyOverly sentimental or tearful.
8. MaverickSSAT vocabularyAn independent-minded person.
9. NonconformistSSAT vocabularyOne who does not follow accepted customs.

19. MicrocosmSSAT vocabularyA small version representing a larger whole.
