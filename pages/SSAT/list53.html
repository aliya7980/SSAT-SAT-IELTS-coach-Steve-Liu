Exercise 3: Paragraph Using All Words

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In one busy school week, every conversation seemed to introduce a new vocabulary word. The company offered Reparation for the damaged packages. Please Replenish the water bottles before practice. The librarian had to Reprove students who were shouting. The old downtown enjoyed a Renaissance after artists opened studios there. One Repercussion of the new rule was longer lunch lines. After the announcement, the office received a Deluge of emails from parents. The captain refused to Relinquish her responsibility after the loss. The usually Reticent student finally shared her idea. Parents came to Remonstrate against the unsafe crossing. The coach gave a quiet Reproach after the careless foul. The school board voted to Repudiate the unfair policy. A signed permission slip was Requisite for the field trip. The pantry was Replete with soup after the food drive. The class learned to Revere the scientist for her courage. The leader decided to Renounce the unfair advantage and play honestly. The hikers enjoyed a brief Respite in the shade. The prank was Reprehensible because it humiliated a younger student.
