Exercise 3: Paragraph Using All Words

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In one busy school week, every conversation seemed to introduce a new vocabulary word. Ms. Rivera became an Expatriate when she moved to Italy for work. To meet the deadline, the clerk tried to Expedite the paperwork. Arguing in the hallway would only Exacerbate the conflict. The instructions were Explicit, leaving no room for confusion. Her honest apology can Exemplify responsible leadership. The principal used the assembly to Extol the students' courage. The applause was Evanescent, disappearing almost as soon as it began. The receipt helped Exonerate Amir after the missing-book accusation. The power outage created an Exigency that required quick action. After the wrong file was submitted, Lina needed help to Extricate herself from the confusing situation. The class used one week of data to Extrapolate the plant's future growth. The coach will Exhort runners to drink water before the race. The detective made an Exhaustive search of the room. The constant tapping began to Exasperate everyone during the test. The phrase "passed away" is a Euphemism for the harsher word "died." The newspaper article sought to Exalt the volunteers who cleaned the beach. The teacher crossed out the Extraneous sentence because it did not support the claim. The scholarship application was Exacting, requiring every detail to be perfect. The novel raised an Existential question about what gives life meaning. The shortcut was Expedient, but it did not solve the deeper problem.
