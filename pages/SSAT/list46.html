Vocabulary List 46: OB-PA Words

1. ObtuseSSAT vocabularySlow to understand; or blunt rather than sharp.
2. OfficiousSSAT vocabularyToo eager to give unwanted help or orders.
3. OnerousSSAT vocabularyBurdensome or difficult.
4. BurdensomeSSAT vocabularyHeavy, troublesome, or hard to bear.
5. OpaqueSSAT vocabularyNot transparent; hard to understand.
6. OpulentSSAT vocabularyWealthy, luxurious, or richly decorated.
7. OrthodoxSSAT vocabularyFollowing accepted or traditional beliefs.
8. OstensibleSSAT vocabularyAppearing to be true, but possibly not.
9. OstentatiousSSAT vocabularyShowy in a way meant to impress.
10. PlacateSSAT vocabularyTo calm or soothe anger.
11. PainstakingSSAT vocabularyDone with great care and effort.
12. PalliateSSAT vocabularyTo make less severe without curing.
13. PalpableSSAT vocabularyAble to be felt; obvious.
14. PaltrySSAT vocabularySmall, weak, or worthless.
15. PanaceaSSAT vocabularyA supposed cure for all problems.
16. ParadigmSSAT vocabularyA model, pattern, or example.
17. ParadoxSSAT vocabularyA statement that seems contradictory but may be true.
18. ParochialSSAT vocabularyNarrow in outlook or limited to local concerns.
19. ParodySSAT vocabularyA humorous imitation.
20. ParsimoniousSSAT vocabularyUnwilling to spend money; stingy.

Exercise 3: Paragraph Using All Words

Read the paragraph and notice how each vocabulary word is used in context.

In one busy school week, every conversation seemed to introduce a new vocabulary word. He was not stupid, but he seemed Obtuse about the obvious joke. Carrying three instruments on the bus was Burdensome for the young musician. The Officious neighbor kept giving advice nobody requested. The school followed an Orthodox calendar with summer vacation. "Less is more" is a familiar Paradox. The school used Finland as a Paradigm for education reform. The hotel lobby looked Opulent with marble floors and gold lamps. The apology helped Placate the angry customer. The apology seemed Paltry after such a serious mistake. The Parsimonious treasurer refused to buy even one pencil. The medicine could Palliate the pain, though it could not cure the illness. The committee's Parochial attitude ignored ideas from other cities. The restoration required Painstaking work with tiny brushes. The Ostensible reason for the meeting was safety, but the real issue was money. The Ostentatious car drew attention on every street. The extra paperwork was Onerous for families already under stress. The tension in the room was Palpable after the vote. The poem seemed Opaque until we discussed its symbols. The new app was advertised as a Panacea, but it could not fix every problem. The drama club wrote a Parody of superhero movies.
