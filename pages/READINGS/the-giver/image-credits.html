Know what each visual represents and where it came from.

Images are illustrations, not evidence of a fictional place

The photographs below show real objects and places. They help introduce an idea; they do not depict the novel’s actual community, characters, or a confirmed destination. Original diagrams organize interpretations and reasoning. Novel events—not actors, photographs, or film scenes—remain the evidence for analysis.

Changes made to photographs

Images are stored locally as compressed WebP files. Snow and library photographs were reduced in size without changing their aspect ratios. The apple remains at its small original resolution; the colour comparison applies a clearly labelled CSS grayscale filter. No image was cropped to imply an event or remove a credit.

Spoilers and adaptations

The apple comparison, snow illustration, plot stages, and character relationships appear only in Full novel analysis. The chapter reading guide uses a neutral library photograph; it does not reveal later events through captions or alt text. No film posters or stills are reproduced. Their public availability was not treated as permission, and no film-only detail has been added to the teaching content.

Maintain these records

The asset-manifest.json file records filenames, creators, source URLs, licence or original-work basis, image dimensions, modifications, and the inspection date. Image permission statements were checked on 2 November 2026. CC0 assets require no attribution; credits are supplied voluntarily. The winter image’s author dedication is recorded on its Commons file page.
