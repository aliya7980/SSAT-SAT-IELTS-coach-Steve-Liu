A HUSKYDADDY NOVEL STUDY

Individuality and Sameness

How can belonging preserve difference rather than erase it?

Essential question

How can belonging preserve difference rather than erase it?

From topic to theme

Topic: difference. A topic names an area of interest; a theme statement argues what the novel suggests about it. A defensible statement allows evidence and qualification. It is not an instruction everyone must obey.

Full-novel analysis is hidden. Choose “Full novel” above when you are ready. Chapter-by-chapter notes remain available in the reading guide.

Two defensible arguments

  • Sameness offers predictable belonging by reducing visible differences, but it also limits the experiences through which people understand themselves and others.
  • Individual perception can begin resistance, although seeing differently does not immediately give a person the language or support needed to act.

These interpretations are starting points, not the novel’s official answers.

How the theme develops

Early comments about eyes establish discomfort with difference. Jonas’s selection distinguishes him publicly. Colour and memory allow him to imagine what has disappeared, but his inability to share those experiences creates loneliness.

Model analytical paragraph

Jonas’s changing perception makes individuality both enriching and isolating. In Chapter 12, learning to name colour gives him a way to understand the strange changes he has noticed. Colour is literally a new part of his experience, but it can also represent alternatives suppressed by Sameness. In Chapter 13, he cannot simply make Asher share that perception. This failed connection matters because the novel does not present individual discovery as an instantly successful solution. Jonas can see more while communicating less effectively with people he likes. His development suggests that preserving difference requires more than exceptional individuals: it also requires relationships and language capable of supporting what those individuals discover.

Original teaching model using paraphrases. Chapter references locate events for study; add page citations from your edition before submitting formal work.

Discuss in increasing depth

  1. Why does Lily’s comment about pale eyes make Jonas uncomfortable?
  2. How is being selected different from choosing an identity?
  3. Can a society honour one exceptional role while still suppressing individuality?

Scaffolded paragraph

Claim that difference brings both ___ and ___. Use an early detail and a later failed connection. Explain the change in Jonas’s understanding.

Use: claim → accurate evidence → explanation of a technique or choice → connection to the theme. Include a qualification such as “although,” “while,” or “does not necessarily.”

Independent writing

Analyse the tension between individuality and belonging without assuming they must always be opposites.

A common misconception

Sameness is not simply everyone dressing alike. It includes decisions about perception, work, relationships, knowledge, and feeling.
