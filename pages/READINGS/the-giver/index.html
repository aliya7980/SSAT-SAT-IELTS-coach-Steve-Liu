A HUSKYDADDY NOVEL STUDY

Start Here

From careful reading to a thoughtful literary argument.

What would you give up to feel safe?

Jonas lives in a community that values order and predictability. As an important childhood ceremony approaches, he begins to notice questions that familiar explanations cannot settle. Read to explore who makes decisions, what people need from one another, and how language shapes what they understand.

This course moves from what happens to how the novel creates meaning. Read the novel first; the notes support rather than replace it.

Your learning route

  1. Read a chapter and record one event, one question, and one detail.
  2. Use the chapter guide without opening chapters you have not read.
  3. After finishing, connect patterns in setting, character, conflict, and technique.
  4. Turn a topic into an arguable theme statement.
  5. Draft a paragraph with evidence, then revise using the rubric.

Learning goals: distinguish observation from inference; explain how literary choices shape meaning; support and qualify interpretations; discuss respectfully; revise writing for a reader. These goals connect with BC English Language Arts 8, rather than covering its entire curriculum.
